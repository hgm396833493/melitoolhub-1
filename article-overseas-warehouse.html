How to Choose an Overseas Warehouse for LATAM E-commerce 2024

📅 December 28, 2023✍️ MeliToolHub⏱ 9 min🌎 Brazil · Mexico

Having local inventory in Latin America is a game-changer for e-commerce sellers. It enables next-day delivery, the coveted Mercado Libre "Full" badge, and drastically reduces the risk of customs delays. But choosing the wrong warehouse model can also drain your cash flow. This guide helps you make the right decision.

Three Main Fulfillment Models

ModelDescriptionBest forMain Risk
Direct ShippingShip from China for each orderTesting phase, <50 orders/monthSlow delivery (15–30 days), customs uncertainty
3PL WarehouseThird-party local warehouse handles storage and dispatchGrowing sellers, 50–500 orders/monthHigher storage cost, contract complexity
MELI Warehouse (MEF)Mercado Libre's own fulfillment centersHigh-volume Mercado Libre-focused sellersLoss of control over packing, only for ML

MELI Warehouse (Mercado Envíos Full)

The official Mercado Libre fulfillment service operates in Brazil, Mexico, Argentina, Chile and Colombia. Key facts:

  • 📦 You send bulk stock to ML's warehouse; ML handles storage, packing, shipping.
  • ⚡ Delivers in 24–48 hours for buyers — qualifies for the "Full" badge.
  • 📈 Listings with "Full" badge typically see 20–35% higher conversion rates.
  • 💰 Fees: storage (~R$3–8/unit/month in Brazil), plus picking/packing fee per order (~R$5–12).
  • ⚠️ Requires CNPJ (Brazil) or RFC (Mexico) for the importer when sending bulk stock.

💡 Recommendation: Start with direct shipping to validate demand. Once you consistently sell 50+ units/month of a product, migrate to a 3PL or MEF for that product specifically.

Risk Management

  • 🔐 Insurance: Always insure shipments >$2,000 USD value. 4PX and most 3PLs offer cargo insurance at ~1-2% of declared value.
  • 📋 Avoid under-declaration: Declaring lower values to save import taxes is illegal and can result in confiscation. The risk is not worth the savings.
  • 🔄 Diversify carriers: Don't rely on a single logistics provider. Have a backup for peak season (Black Friday, Christmas).
  • ⚖️ Compliance first: Products without proper certifications (INMETRO, ANVISA) will be seized even after reaching the local warehouse.
